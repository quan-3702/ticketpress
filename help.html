TicketPress – How to use

Print numbered raffle tickets and event tickets with tear-off stubs.

1. Choose the kind of ticket2. Set the numbers3. Write the words4. Design, colour and paper5. Print and cut6. Add your logo (Pro)7. QR code and drawing a winner8. Free version and Pro9. If something goes wrong

1. Choose the kind of ticket

  • Raffle tickets: the stub on the left has lines for the buyer's name and phone number. You keep the stub and put it in the draw; the buyer keeps the ticket.
  • Event tickets: the stub says ADMIT ONE with the event name and date. Tear it off at the door.
Open ☰ More and choose an example to see how it works, then change the words.

2. Set the numbers

  1. First number: usually 1.
  2. How many tickets: for example 200.
  3. Text before the number: for example No. or #. Leave it empty for numbers only.

Every ticket and its stub get the same number, so you can always match them. The line under the boxes shows which numbers will be printed and how many sheets of paper you need.

Need more tickets later? Click Continue numbering after this batch. The first number jumps to the next free number, so no number is printed twice.

3. Write the words

  • Event name, Organised by, Date, Time, Place and Price – leave any box empty if you do not need it.
  • Extra line: for example the grand prize, or "Doors open at 7:00 PM".
  • Lines on the stub (raffle only): one per line, for example Name, Phone, Email.

Long words are made a little smaller so they always fit on the ticket.

4. Design, colour and paper

  • Click one of the small pictures to choose a design, and a coloured circle for the colour.
  • Paper: US Letter or A4.
  • Tickets per sheet: 8 large, 10, or 16 small tickets.

5. Print and cut

  1. Click 🖨 Print.
  2. In the print window set Scale to 100% and Margins to None or Default.
  3. Turn on Background graphics so the coloured designs print.
  4. Cut along the light dotted lines around each ticket. Fold along the dotted line between the stub and the ticket before tearing, or cut it with scissors.
Thicker paper (card stock) makes sturdier tickets. A paper trimmer makes cutting many tickets much faster.

6. Add your logo (Pro)

Click Add a logo picture… and choose a picture from your PC. It is printed in the corner of every ticket. Click Remove logo to take it away.

7. QR code and drawing a winner

  • QR code (Pro): on event tickets, tick QR code. Each ticket gets a QR code with its own number, so you can scan tickets at the door with any phone camera.
  • 🎲 Draw a winner: open ☰ More and choose Draw a winner. TicketPress picks a random number from the tickets you printed. A number that was drawn is never drawn again, so you can draw 1st, 2nd and 3rd prizes one after another. Click Full screen to show the number to the room.
The draw uses your computer's secure random generator, so every ticket has the same chance.

8. Free version and Pro

The free version never limits how often you print.

  • Free: 3 designs, up to 100 numbered tickets at a time (print the next 100 with Continue numbering).
  • Pro (one-time purchase): all 8 designs, up to 5000 tickets at a time, your own logo, QR codes on event tickets.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The colours are missing: turn on Background graphics in the print window.
  • The tickets are smaller or larger than expected: set Scale to 100%.
  • The edge of the page is cut off: the printer cannot print to the very edge. The tickets keep an 8 mm margin, which suits most printers.
  • My texts are gone on another PC: they are stored only on the PC where you typed them.
